## What a MIDI file is for

MIDI is a list of notes for music software and instruments to play back. You
open it in a recording program, put a virtual instrument on it and edit it. It
is the right choice if you produce music, arrange in a program like a digital
audio workstation, or want to drive a keyboard or synthesiser from software.

Opened on its own, a MIDI file is usually shown as a piano roll: bars on a
grid. That is easy for software and hard to read while you play.

## What tonic solfa is for

Tonic solfa is for a person to read and play. Each note is a syllable, do re
mi fa so la ti, that says where the note sits in the key. If you learned music
in a choir or a church, it may be the notation you already know.

## Which one to choose

| You want to | Choose |
|---|---|
| Learn to play or sing a song | Tonic solfa |
| Teach a tune to a choir or a class | Tonic solfa |
| Edit the notes in music software | MIDI |
| Replace the sound with a different instrument | MIDI |

## You can have both

[Song to tonic solfa](/song-to-tonic-solfa) writes the solfa first, and every
song can also be downloaded as MIDI, with the melody on one track and the
chords on another, at the exact times they were sung. Open it in a keyboard
app, a recording program or MuseScore. MusicXML is there too for notation
programs, and the staff notation shows the same tune on a stave.

The solfa and the MIDI come from the same notes, so if a note is wrong in one
it is wrong in the other. Check it by ear first: the player can play the
detected notes on a piano sound next to the original.
